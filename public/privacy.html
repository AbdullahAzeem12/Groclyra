Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Applies to the Groclyra Android app and https://groclyra.web.app

Groclyra ("we", "us", operated by Abdullah Azeem, Lahore, Pakistan) operates an online grocery marketplace. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights. Using any Groclyra service means you agree to this policy. For any privacy matter contact us first at kheroali66@gmail.com or +92 321 7649976.

1. Who we are

Groclyra is a grocery marketplace based in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan operating under the name "Groclyra" as a sole proprietorship. We are the controller of the data described here.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash), delivery address and preferred delivery zone.
  • Order data: the items you buy, delivery address, order notes, and payment references.
  • Location data: your device GPS location, only when the app is running and you are using the live order-tracking feature. You can deny the location permission and still use the app (with manual address entry).
  • Payment data: we never see or store your full card number, CVV or wallet PIN. Online payments are processed by licensed payment gateways (Premier PayFast) and mobile wallets (JazzCash / Easypaisa) on their own secure pages. We receive only a transaction reference and a status (paid / failed).
  • Device & technical data: Firebase services (Google LLC) handle authentication, database hosting, crash analytics and push notification infrastructure on our behalf. Existing Firebase policies apply to your Firebase data.
  • Seller/rider KYC data: sellers and delivery riders must verify their identity (CNIC details, bank account). This is used only to run the marketplace and pay out earnings, and is visible only to Groclyra administrators.

3. Why we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and deliver orders you place.
  • To pass the delivery address and order contents to the seller and rider fulfilling your order.
  • To collect payment through licensed payment providers and issue refunds where applicable.
  • To notify you about your order status (by in-app notifications, SMS where supported, and optional push notifications you can disable in your device settings).
  • To detect fraud, enforce our Terms & Conditions, and comply with lawful requests from Pakistani authorities (e.g. SBP, FBR) or courts.

4. What we NEVER do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not send marketing SMS without your consent; you can opt out of promotional notifications at any time.
  • We do not store money in your account; online payments go directly to our licensed merchant accounts and are paid out to sellers/settled by our PSP.

5. Who we share data with, and why

  • Sellers: receive only the details required to pack your order (items, expiry notes, order number) — never your identity or financial data.
  • Riders: receive your first name, the delivery address and phone number during an active delivery, for the purpose of completing that delivery only.
  • Payment processors: Premier PayFast (SBP-licensed PSO/PSO&PS) and mobile-wallet processors receive what is required to process your payment (amount, order reference). Their own privacy policies apply additionally to data they process on their platforms.
  • Infrastructure: Firebase / Google Cloud hosting (databases and hosting under our project IDs). Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure; a copy of your account record and orders may be replicated in data centres outside Pakistan, protected by the contract terms of Firebase / Google Cloud services.
  • Regulators: where the law requires (e.g., the State Bank of Pakistan, FBR, a court order), we can disclose data.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Access/Correction: You can view and edit your name, phone, and address in the app's Profile section, or request us by email.
  • Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and personal data at kheroali66@gmail.com. We will delete or irreversibly anonymise your profile and personal data within 30 days, except for records we must keep under Pakistani law (payment-history records kept by the licensed PSP and basic financial audit records).
  • Complaints: If you are in Punjab, you may contact the Punjab Commission on the Status of Women helpline 1043 for guidance on your data-rights concerns, or the relevant authority under the PECA framework; we also ask you to contact us first so we can fix the issue directly.

7. Children

Groclyra is for users aged 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; payment credentials never touch our servers. Access to customer data within Groclyra is limited to roles that need it (customer service and moderators). Despite best efforts, no internet transmission is 100% secure — please keep your device and account password safe.

9. Policy changes

We will update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the current version. Material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
