Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Because groceries are perishable, we ask customers to inspect their order at the doorstep. This policy explains when and how you can return an item and how refunds are issued. It applies to every order placed through the Groclyra app or website.

1. Doorstep inspection (please check at handover)

Our riders hand over your order with a delivery verification step (OTP/signature). At the moment of handover you may reject items that are visibly damaged, expired, leaking or wrong — the rider records the rejection on the spot. This is the fastest way to resolve an issue.

2. What can be returned

Item conditionAction window
Damaged, leaking, expired or visibly spoiled at handoverImmediately at the door (rejection recorded by the rider)
Wrong item delivered / item missingWithin 12 hours of delivery via the app or phone
Fresh item (fruit, vegetables, dairy, bakery) found inferior/rotten after openingWithin 12 hours, with a photo of the item
Packaged / non-perishable goods with quality or seal issuesWithin 48 hours, item unused and packaging intact

3. What cannot be returned

  • Perishable items that were accepted at the door and later found partially used, cooked or stored improperly.
  • Items without proof of purchase (order number or receipt).
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse (e.g. refrigerated products left out of the fridge for long periods).
  • Free promotional items or items marked "final sale" for clearance.

4. How to request a return

  1. Open your order in the Groclyra app Profile → My Orders → select the order and use the report option, or
  2. Call/WhatsApp us at +92 321 7649976 (10:00 AM – 8:00 PM, Mon–Sat), or email kheroali66@gmail.com with your order number and a photo of the problem item where applicable.

Our support team responds within 24 hours and arranges a pickup or resolves by refund, depending on the case.

5. Refund methods and timelines

Original payment methodRefund goes toTimeline
Cash on DeliveryNext order credit, cash at re-pickup, or bank/wallet transferAs agreed with you (usually instant to 3 working days)
Card / online payment via our payment gatewayRefunded back to the same card/bank account through the gateway7 to 14 working days (your bank may add 1–2 days; timelines ultimately per bank policy)
Mobile wallet (JazzCash / Easypaisa)Back to the same wallet account3 to 7 working days

Partial refunds apply when only part of an order is returned (e.g. one item out of many). Delivery fee is refunded in full when the entire order is being returned due to our mistake; it is not refunded when returning a single unwanted-but-fine item.

6. Order cancellations

  • You can cancel an unpaid order any time from the app while it has not yet been packed.
  • For a paid order that has not been packed/dispatched, cancellation is instant and the payment is refunded per the methods above. Once the rider has picked up your order, cancel as a return request instead.

7. Approved refunds are always documented

Every refund decision is recorded against your order with the reason and amount, so you can always ask us for a written confirmation by email for your records.

8. Contact for disputes

If you are not satisfied with how a refund was handled, write to kheroali66@gmail.com with subject "Refund Dispute — [order number]". A Groclyra team member (Abdullah Azeem) personally reviews disputes and replies within 48 hours, and you may escalate via PayFast's merchant dispute process where a technical gateway issue is suspected.
