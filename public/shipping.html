Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Groclyra delivers groceries to customers in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. This page explains our delivery areas, timings, charges and what to expect when your order is on the way.

1. Delivery areas

We currently deliver within Lahore city limits (all major residential and commercial zones, including DHA, Model Town, Gulberg, Johar Town, Bahria Town, Wapda Town, Township, Cantt and nearby housing societies). If your pin is outside our current zone the app/phone team will tell you before payment is taken. We are actively expanding to more cities.

2. Order processing times

  • Same-day delivery: orders placed between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM are packed by the seller and dispatched the same day, subject to rider availability.
  • Evening / late orders: placed after 6:00 PM are delivered the next morning.
  • Peak hours & weather: heavy rain, political events or other emergencies can add 30–90 minutes to the estimate; the app shows any revised ETA automatically.

3. What the customer tracks

You receive live status updates in the app: order confirmed → seller packing → rider assigned → out for delivery → delivered, with the rider's live location on the map while your order is in transit.

4. Delivery charges

Order valueDelivery fee
Below Rs. 500Rs. 119 flat
Rs. 1,000 – Rs. 2,499Rs. 79 flat
Rs. 2,500 and aboveFree delivery

Prices shown at checkout always include the delivery fee before you confirm payment — there are no hidden charges added later.

5. Proof of delivery

Every handover is OTP / signature-verified against your phone number, so an order can never be dropped with the wrong person. If you are not available, the rider will call you; after two failed attempts the order is returned to the seller and dealt with under our Return & Refund Policy.

6. Packaging & cold chain

  • Dairy, frozen and chilled items are bagged separately and delivered the same day they are purchased from the seller.
  • Fragile items (glass bottles, eggs) are packed with protective lining. If anything arrives damaged, reject it at the door or report within 12 hours per the Return & Refund Policy.

7. Failed or delayed deliveries

If your order is late beyond the quoted window, contact us at +92 321 7649976 — late deliveries beyond 60 minutes qualify for a delivery-fee waiver on that order. Repeated failed deliveries due to wrong address/phone provided by the customer may be charged the actual return trip cost, which we will always communicate before charging.

8. Address & contact accuracy

Please keep your saved delivery address and phone number up to date in the app. Groclyra is not responsible for failed deliveries caused by inaccurate addresses or unreachable phone numbers.

9. Support hours

Our support line (+92 321 7649976, WhatsApp available) is open Monday–Saturday, 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
